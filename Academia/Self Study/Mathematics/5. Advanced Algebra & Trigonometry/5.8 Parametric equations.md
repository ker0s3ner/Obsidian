## Definition

A curve given by expressing $x$ and $y$ separately as functions of a third variable $t$, the **parameter**:

$$x = f(t), \qquad y = g(t)$$

**Example — a circle of radius $a$:**

$$x = a\cos t, \qquad y = a\sin t, \qquad 0 \leq t \leq 2\pi$$

---

## Eliminating the Parameter

To recover a Cartesian equation, solve for $t$ and substitute, or use an identity.

**Example:**

$$x = t + 1, \quad y = t^2 \implies t = x - 1 \implies y = (x-1)^2$$

---

## Slope of a Parametric Curve

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}$$

**Example — for $x = t^2$, $y = t^3$:**

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{3t^2}{2t} = \frac{3t}{2}$$

> [!warning] Undefined slope
> When $dx/dt = 0$ but $dy/dt \neq 0$, the curve has a vertical tangent at that point.

## Practice Problems

1. Eliminate the parameter from $x = 2t - 1$, $y = t^2 + 3$.
2. Find $dy/dx$ for $x = \cos t$, $y = \sin 2t$ at $t = \pi/4$.
3. Sketch the curve $x = t^2 - 1$, $y = t^3 - t$ for $-2 \leq t \leq 2$.
4. Show that $x = \cosh t$, $y = \sinh t$ traces a branch of the hyperbola $x^2 - y^2 = 1$.
