## Product Norms

For normed spaces $X, Y$, the product $X \times Y$ can be normed several equivalent ways:

$$\|(x,y)\|_1 = \|x\| + \|y\|, \quad \|(x,y)\|_2 = \sqrt{\|x\|^2 + \|y\|^2}, \quad \|(x,y)\|_\infty = \max(\|x\|, \|y\|)$$

All are equivalent (finite product), and each induces the product topology.

---

## Quotient Norms

If $M \subseteq X$ is a closed subspace, the **quotient norm** on $X/M$ is:

$$\|x + M\| = \inf_{m \in M} \|x - m\|$$

i.e. the distance from $x$ to $M$.

> [!warning] $M$ must be closed
> If $M$ is not closed, $\inf_{m \in M}\|x-m\| = 0$ for $x$ in the closure of $M$ but $x \notin M$, so the quotient "norm" fails positivity — it's only a norm once $M$ is closed.

**Fact**: if $X$ is Banach and $M$ is closed, then $X/M$ is Banach.

## Practice Problems

1. Verify that $\|(x,y)\|_1$, $\|(x,y)\|_2$, $\|(x,y)\|_\infty$ are pairwise equivalent on $X \times Y$.
2. Show the quotient norm satisfies the triangle inequality.
3. Let $M = \{f \in C[0,1] : f(0) = 0\}$. Compute $\|g + M\|$ for $g(t) = t + 1$.
