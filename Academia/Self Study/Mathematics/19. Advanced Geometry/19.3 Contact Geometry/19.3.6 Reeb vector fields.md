## Definition

Given a contact form $\alpha$, the **Reeb vector field** $R_\alpha$ is the unique vector field satisfying:

$$\alpha(R_\alpha) = 1, \qquad d\alpha(R_\alpha, \cdot) = 0$$

It is transverse to the contact planes $\xi$ everywhere and depends on the specific form $\alpha$, not just the structure $\xi$.

---

## Dynamics

Flow lines of $R_\alpha$ are **Reeb orbits**. The existence of *periodic* Reeb orbits is a deep question with major consequences:

> [!example] Weinstein conjecture
> Every contact form on a closed manifold has at least one periodic Reeb orbit — proved in dimension 3 by Taubes (2007) using Seiberg–Witten theory, and open in general in higher dimensions.

---

## Example on the Standard Sphere

On $S^3$ with its standard contact form, all Reeb orbits are periodic — this special case (the **Hopf fibration**) is the model example motivating the general conjecture.

## Practice Problems

1. Compute $R_\alpha$ explicitly for $\alpha = dz - y\,dx$ on $\mathbb{R}^3$.
2. Explain why $R_\alpha$ is always transverse to $\xi$.
3. State the Weinstein conjecture and its status in dimension 3 vs. higher dimensions.
4. Describe how the Hopf fibration arises as the Reeb flow on $S^3$.
