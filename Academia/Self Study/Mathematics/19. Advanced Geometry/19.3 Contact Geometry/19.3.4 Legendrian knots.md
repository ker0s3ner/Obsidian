## Definition

A **Legendrian knot** is a Legendrian submanifold of $(\mathbb{R}^3, \alpha=dz-y\,dx)$ diffeomorphic to a circle — a knot that is everywhere tangent to the contact planes, not just any smoothly embedded circle.

---

## Classical Invariants

Beyond the underlying knot type, Legendrian knots carry two extra invariants:

| Invariant | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Thurston–Bennequin number $tb$ | Self-linking measured via the contact framing |
| Rotation number $r$ | Winding of the tangent vector in the contact planes |

Two Legendrian knots with the same underlying knot type can be **non-Legendrian-isotopic** even with matching $(tb, r)$ — Legendrian knot theory is strictly finer than ordinary knot theory.

> [!example] Legendrian trefoils
> The standard trefoil admits multiple non-isotopic Legendrian representatives distinguished only by finer invariants beyond $(tb, r)$ — discovered via **Legendrian contact homology**.

## Practice Problems

1. Compute $tb$ and $r$ for the Legendrian unknot from its standard front diagram.
2. Explain why stabilization (adding a zigzag to the front) always decreases $tb$ by 1.
3. State why Legendrian isotopy is strictly finer than smooth isotopy of the underlying knot.
4. Research: what is Legendrian contact homology, and what does it distinguish?
