## Overview

Geometries beyond the Euclidean plane, organized by which axiom or structure is relaxed: **non-Euclidean geometry** drops the parallel postulate, **projective geometry** drops distance altogether in favor of incidence, and **contact geometry** studies the odd-dimensional analogue of symplectic structure.
