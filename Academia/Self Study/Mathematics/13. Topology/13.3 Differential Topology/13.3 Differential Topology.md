## Overview

Differential topology studies smooth manifolds and smooth maps between them, using calculus rather than purely set-theoretic open covers. It bridges [[13. Topology]]'s point-set foundations and the geometric machinery of [[4.11 Riemannian Geometry]].

> [!info] Prerequisites
> - [[13.1 Topology I]] (topological spaces, continuity)
> - Multivariable calculus ([[6.3 Calculus III]])

## Major Theorems

| Theorem | Statement |
|---------|-----------|
| Sard | Critical values have measure zero |
| Morse Lemma | Non-degenerate critical points look quadratic locally |
| Whitney Embedding | Every $n$-manifold embeds in $\mathbb{R}^{2n}$ |
| Tubular Neighborhood | Every closed submanifold has a normal-bundle neighborhood |
