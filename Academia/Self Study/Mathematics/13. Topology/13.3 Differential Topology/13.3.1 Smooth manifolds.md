## Definition

A **smooth manifold** of dimension $n$ is a Hausdorff, second-countable topological space $M$ with an **atlas** of charts $\{(U_\alpha, \varphi_\alpha)\}$, where each $\varphi_\alpha : U_\alpha \to \mathbb{R}^n$ is a homeomorphism onto an open subset, and transition maps:

$$\varphi_\beta \circ \varphi_\alpha^{-1} : \varphi_\alpha(U_\alpha \cap U_\beta) \to \varphi_\beta(U_\alpha \cap U_\beta)$$

are $C^\infty$ (smooth) wherever defined.

---

## Tangent Spaces

At each $p \in M$, the **tangent space** $T_pM$ is an $n$-dimensional vector space of "velocity vectors" of curves through $p$, defined intrinsically (independent of any chart) as equivalence classes of curves, or as linear derivations on smooth functions.

> [!example] Standard examples
> - $\mathbb{R}^n$ itself, with the identity chart
> - The sphere $S^n \subset \mathbb{R}^{n+1}$, covered by two stereographic-projection charts
> - Any open subset of a manifold

---

## Smooth Maps

$f : M \to N$ is **smooth** if $\psi \circ f \circ \varphi^{-1}$ is smooth for all charts $\varphi$ on $M$, $\psi$ on $N$. The **differential** $df_p : T_pM \to T_{f(p)}N$ is the linearization of $f$ at $p$.

## Practice Problems

1. Construct an explicit smooth atlas for $S^1$ using two charts.
2. Show that transition maps between overlapping stereographic projection charts on $S^n$ are smooth.
3. Explain why the tangent space definition via curves is independent of choice of chart.
4. Show that the differential $df_p$ is a linear map.
5. Verify that a composition of smooth maps between manifolds is smooth.
