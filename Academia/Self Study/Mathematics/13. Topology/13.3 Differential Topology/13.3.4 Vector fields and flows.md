## Vector Fields

A **vector field** on a manifold $M$ assigns to each $p \in M$ a tangent vector $X(p) \in T_pM$, smoothly in $p$.

---

## Flows

The **flow** of $X$ is the family of maps $\phi_t : M \to M$ solving:

$$\frac{d}{dt}\phi_t(p) = X(\phi_t(p)), \qquad \phi_0(p) = p$$

By the existence/uniqueness theorem for ODEs, this exists locally in $t$ for smooth $X$; on a compact manifold it exists for all $t \in \mathbb{R}$, forming a one-parameter group of diffeomorphisms.

> [!example] Rotation flow
> On $S^1$, the vector field $X = \partial_\theta$ has flow $\phi_t(\theta) = \theta + t$ — rotation.

---

## Lie Bracket

Two vector fields $X, Y$ have a **Lie bracket** $[X,Y]$, itself a vector field, measuring the failure of their flows to commute:

$$[X,Y]f = X(Yf) - Y(Xf)$$

$$[X,Y] = 0 \iff \phi^X_s \circ \phi^Y_t = \phi^Y_t \circ \phi^X_s \text{ for all } s,t$$

## Practice Problems

1. Compute the flow of the vector field $X = x\,\partial_x$ on $\mathbb{R} \setminus \{0\}$.
2. Show that the flow of a vector field on a compact manifold exists for all time.
3. Compute $[X,Y]$ for $X = \partial_x$, $Y = x\partial_y$ on $\mathbb{R}^2$.
4. Explain why $[X,Y] = 0$ corresponds to commuting flows.
