## Definition

For a submanifold $S \subseteq M$, a **tubular neighborhood** is an open neighborhood $U \supseteq S$ diffeomorphic to the total space of the **normal bundle** $\nu(S)$ of $S$ in $M$, via a diffeomorphism restricting to the identity on $S$.

Intuitively: near $S$, $M$ looks like $S$ "thickened" by the directions normal to it.

---

## Tubular Neighborhood Theorem

Every compact (or, more generally, closed) submanifold of a manifold has a tubular neighborhood. The construction uses the **exponential map** of a Riemannian metric: fire off short geodesics in normal directions from each point of $S$.

> [!tip] Why this is useful
> Tubular neighborhoods let you replace a submanifold by its normal bundle for local computations — the backbone of transversality arguments, intersection theory, and surgery constructions.
