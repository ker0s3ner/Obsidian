## Group Actions on Spaces

A group $G$ **acts** on a topological space $X$ if each $g \in G$ gives a homeomorphism of $X$, compatibly with composition. The **quotient space** $X/G$ inherits geometric structure from $X$.

---

## Covering Space Actions

If $G$ acts **freely and properly discontinuously** on $X$, then $X \to X/G$ is a covering map, and:

$$\pi_1(X/G) \cong G \quad \text{if } X \text{ is simply connected}$$

> [!example] Standard examples
> - $\mathbb{Z}^n$ acting on $\mathbb{R}^n$ by translation gives quotient $T^n$
> - $\mathbb{Z}/2$ acting on $S^n$ by the antipodal map gives quotient $\mathbb{R}P^n$

---

## Practice Problems

1. Verify that $\mathbb{Z}$ acting on $\mathbb{R}$ by translation gives quotient $S^1$.
2. Show that the antipodal action of $\mathbb{Z}/2$ on $S^n$ is free.
3. Explain why properly discontinuous, free actions are exactly the ones giving covering-space quotients.
