## Connected Sum

Given two manifolds $M, N$ of the same dimension, remove a ball from each and glue along the resulting boundary spheres:

$$M \# N = (M \setminus D^n) \cup_{\partial} (N \setminus D^n)$$

The connected sum with a sphere is trivial: $M \# S^n \cong M$.

---

## Decomposition Results

| Dimension | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| 2 (surfaces) | Every closed orientable surface is $\#_g T^2$ (connected sum of $g$ tori) |
| 3 | Prime decomposition into indecomposable pieces (see [[13.4.1 3-manifolds]]) |

> [!tip] Genus as a connected-sum invariant
> The genus of a connected sum of surfaces adds: $g(\Sigma_1 \# \Sigma_2) = g(\Sigma_1) + g(\Sigma_2)$.

## Practice Problems

1. Show that connected sum with $S^n$ leaves a manifold unchanged up to homeomorphism.
2. Verify the genus-addition formula for connected sums of a torus and a genus-2 surface.
3. Explain why prime decomposition for 3-manifolds is analogous to unique factorization of integers.
