## Definition

The **braid group** $B_n$ is generated by $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_{n-1}$ (crossing strand $i$ over $i+1$), subject to:

$$\sigma_i \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i = \sigma_{i+1}\sigma_i \sigma_{i+1}, \qquad \sigma_i\sigma_j = \sigma_j\sigma_i \ (|i-j| \geq 2)$$

Elements correspond to $n$-strand braid diagrams up to isotopy.

---

## Connection to Knots

Closing up a braid (connecting top strand $i$ to bottom strand $i$) produces a knot or link. By **Alexander's theorem**, every knot or link arises this way, making braid groups a combinatorial tool for studying knots (see [[13.4.2 Knot theory]]).

## Practice Problems

1. Verify the braid relation $\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_1 = \sigma_2\sigma_1\sigma_2$ diagrammatically for $B_3$.
2. Describe the closure of the braid $\sigma_1 \in B_2$ and identify the resulting knot.
3. Explain informally why $B_n$ surjects onto the symmetric group $S_n$ (forgetting over/under crossing information).
