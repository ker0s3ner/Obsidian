## Uniformization

Every closed orientable surface $\Sigma_g$ carries a metric of constant curvature, determined entirely by genus:

| Genus | Constant curvature geometry |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| $g = 0$ | Spherical ($+1$) |
| $g = 1$ | Euclidean ($0$) |
| $g \geq 2$ | Hyperbolic ($-1$) |

This is the **Uniformization Theorem**, and it means "most" surfaces (genus $\geq 2$) are hyperbolic.

---

## Gauss–Bonnet Consistency

$$\int_\Sigma K \, dA = 2\pi \chi(\Sigma)$$

For $g \geq 2$, $\chi(\Sigma) = 2-2g < 0$, forcing $K < 0$ somewhere — consistent with hyperbolic geometry being the only constant-curvature option available.
