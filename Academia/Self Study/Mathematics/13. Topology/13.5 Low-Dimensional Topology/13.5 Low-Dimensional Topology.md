## Overview

Low-dimensional topology focuses on dimensions 2, 3, and 4, where geometric intuition, combinatorics, and algebra interact especially tightly — surfaces are fully classified, 3-manifolds have deep geometric structure, and dimension 4 remains full of open problems.
