## Definition

A **Heegaard splitting** decomposes a closed orientable 3-manifold $M$ into two handlebodies $H_1, H_2$ glued along their common boundary surface $\Sigma$:

$$M = H_1 \cup_\Sigma H_2$$

The **genus** of the splitting is the genus of $\Sigma$.

---

## Existence

Every closed orientable 3-manifold admits a Heegaard splitting (built from a triangulation or Morse function), so this gives a completely combinatorial way to encode any 3-manifold via a surface plus gluing data.

> [!example] Simplest cases
> - $S^3$ has a genus-0 Heegaard splitting: two balls glued along $S^2$.
> - Lens spaces $L(p,q)$ have genus-1 splittings, encoded by gluing two solid tori via a $(p,q)$ map on the boundary torus.
