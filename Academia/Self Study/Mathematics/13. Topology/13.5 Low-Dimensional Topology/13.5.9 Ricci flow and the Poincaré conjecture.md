## Ricci Flow

The **Ricci flow** evolves a Riemannian metric $g$ on a manifold by:

$$\frac{\partial g}{\partial t} = -2\,\text{Ric}(g)$$

smoothing out curvature irregularities over time, analogous to the heat equation smoothing temperature.

---

## Perelman's Proof

> [!example] Resolving Thurston's Geometrization Conjecture
> Perelman used Ricci flow **with surgery** — running the flow, performing controlled topological surgeries when singularities form, and continuing — to prove Thurston's Geometrization Conjecture (see [[13.4.1 3-manifolds]]), which includes the **Poincaré Conjecture** as a special case.

The key technical tools were Perelman's **monotonicity formulas** (entropy functionals that control the flow's long-term behavior) and detailed analysis of singularity formation.
