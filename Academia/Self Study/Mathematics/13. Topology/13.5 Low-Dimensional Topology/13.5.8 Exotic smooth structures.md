## Definition

Two smooth structures on the same topological manifold are **exotic** relative to each other if the underlying spaces are homeomorphic but **not diffeomorphic** — same topology, genuinely different smooth geometry.

---

## Exotic $\mathbb{R}^4$

> [!example] A uniquely 4-dimensional phenomenon
> $\mathbb{R}^4$ admits uncountably many pairwise non-diffeomorphic smooth structures ("exotic $\mathbb{R}^4$s") — this is completely unique to dimension 4. Every other $\mathbb{R}^n$ has a unique smooth structure.

This phenomenon, along with exotic structures on closed 4-manifolds, comes from Donaldson and Seiberg–Witten invariants distinguishing smooth structures that Freedman's classification cannot see.
