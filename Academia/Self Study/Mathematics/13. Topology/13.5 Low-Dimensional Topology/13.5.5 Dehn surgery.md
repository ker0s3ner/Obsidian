## Definition

Given a knot $K \subset S^3$, **Dehn surgery** removes a tubular neighborhood of $K$ (a solid torus) and glues it back with a twist specified by a **slope** $p/q \in \mathbb{Q} \cup \{\infty\}$, producing a new closed 3-manifold.

---

## Why It's Fundamental

> [!tip] Every 3-manifold arises this way
> The **Lickorish–Wallace theorem**: every closed orientable 3-manifold can be obtained by Dehn surgery on some link in $S^3$. This makes surgery diagrams a universal, finite way to describe 3-manifolds.

**Example**: surgery with slope $\infty$ on the unknot recovers $S^3$ unchanged; surgery with slope $1/n$ can produce lens spaces.
