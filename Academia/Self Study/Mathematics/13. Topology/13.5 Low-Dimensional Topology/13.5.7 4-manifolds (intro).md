## Why Dimension 4 Is Special

In dimensions $\geq 5$, smooth and topological classification of manifolds largely align (surgery theory works cleanly). In dimension 4, this breaks down completely — 4-manifolds are simultaneously the most geometrically rigid and the least classifiable.

---

## Key Phenomena

> [!example] Two landmark facts
> - **Freedman's theorem**: closed simply connected topological 4-manifolds are classified by their intersection form on $H_2$.
> - **Donaldson's theorem**: not every unimodular symmetric bilinear form is realized by a *smooth* 4-manifold — smooth and topological classification diverge sharply.

This gap is what produces **exotic smooth structures** (see [[13.5.8 Exotic smooth structures]]): manifolds homeomorphic but not diffeomorphic.
