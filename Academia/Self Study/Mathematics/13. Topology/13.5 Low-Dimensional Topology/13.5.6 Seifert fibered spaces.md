## Definition

A **Seifert fibered space** is a 3-manifold foliated by circles (fibers), all but finitely many of which look locally like a trivial $S^1$-bundle; the exceptional fibers have a "twisted" neighborhood classified by a pair of coprime integers.

---

## Role in Geometrization

Seifert fibered spaces form one of the eight model geometries in Thurston's geometrization (see [[13.4.1 3-manifolds]]) — they are exactly the 3-manifolds admitting a "nice" circle-bundle-like structure, and many small, well-understood 3-manifolds (lens spaces, most surgeries on torus knots) fall into this class.
