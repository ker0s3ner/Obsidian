## Motivation

A **spectral sequence** is a computational device: a sequence of pages $E_r^{p,q}$, each obtained from the previous by taking homology of a differential $d_r$, that converges to the homology or cohomology of a space built from a filtration (e.g. a fiber bundle).

$$E_r^{p,q} \Rightarrow H^{p+q}(X)$$

Think of it as a vast generalization of Mayer–Vietoris, handling filtrations with many layers rather than just two overlapping pieces.

---

## The Serre Spectral Sequence

For a fibration $F \to E \to B$ with $B$ simply connected:

$$E_2^{p,q} = H^p(B; H^q(F)) \Rightarrow H^{p+q}(E)$$

> [!tip] Why "converges"
> The sequence stabilizes after finitely many pages (for finite-dimensional spaces), and the final page's entries assemble (via associated graded pieces) into $H^*(E)$.
