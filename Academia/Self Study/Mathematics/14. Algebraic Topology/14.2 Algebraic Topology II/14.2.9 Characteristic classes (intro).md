## Definition

A **characteristic class** assigns to each vector bundle $E \to B$ a cohomology class $c(E) \in H^*(B)$, natural under pullback: $c(f^*E) = f^*c(E)$. They measure "how twisted" a bundle is — obstructions to it being trivial.

---

## Main Examples

| Class | Bundle type | Lives in |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Euler class $e(E)$ | Oriented real bundles | $H^n(B)$ |
| Chern classes $c_i(E)$ | Complex bundles | $H^{2i}(B)$ |
| Pontryagin classes $p_i(E)$ | Real bundles | $H^{4i}(B)$ |

> [!tip] Detecting nontriviality
> A bundle with nonzero characteristic class cannot be trivial — e.g. the tangent bundle of $S^2$ has nonzero Euler class $e(TS^2) = 2 \in H^2(S^2)$ (related to the hairy ball theorem: no nonvanishing vector field on $S^2$).
