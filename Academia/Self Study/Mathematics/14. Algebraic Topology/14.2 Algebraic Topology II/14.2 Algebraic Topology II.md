## Overview

Where [[14.1 Algebraic Topology I]] uses homotopy (fundamental group, covering spaces), this section builds the **homological** toolkit — abelian invariants that are often far easier to compute than homotopy groups, at the cost of losing some information.

> [!info] Prerequisites
> - [[14.1 Algebraic Topology I]]
> - [[15.1 Group Theory]] (abelian groups, quotients)
