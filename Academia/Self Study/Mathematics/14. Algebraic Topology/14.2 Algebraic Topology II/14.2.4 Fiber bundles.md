## Definition

A **fiber bundle** $p : E \to B$ with fiber $F$ is locally trivial: every $b \in B$ has a neighborhood $U$ with $p^{-1}(U) \cong U \times F$, compatibly with $p$. Unlike a fibration, this local triviality is a genuine geometric condition, not just up-to-homotopy.

---

## Examples

> [!example] Standard bundles
> - **Trivial bundle**: $E = B \times F$
> - **Möbius band**: line bundle over $S^1$, non-trivial (the fiber "flips" going around)
> - **Hopf fibration**: $S^1 \to S^3 \to S^2$
> - **Tangent bundle** $TM \to M$: fiber $T_pM$ over each point

---

## Structure Group

A bundle's **structure group** $G$ records how fibers are glued across overlapping trivializations, via transition functions $U_\alpha \cap U_\beta \to G$ acting on $F$. This links fiber bundles to [[16. Specialized Topics/Lie Groups & Lie Algebras|Lie groups]] and to classifying spaces (see [[14.1.7 Classifying spaces (intro)]]).

## Practice Problems

1. Show that the Möbius band is a nontrivial line bundle over $S^1$ by exhibiting the twisted transition function.
2. Explain why every fiber bundle is a fibration (satisfies the homotopy lifting property).
3. Describe the structure group of the tangent bundle of a smooth $n$-manifold.
