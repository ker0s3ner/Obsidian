## Presheaves

A **presheaf** of abelian groups on a topological space $X$ assigns a group $\mathcal{F}(U)$ to every open $U \subseteq X$, and **restriction maps** $\mathcal{F}(U) \to \mathcal{F}(V)$ for $V \subseteq U$, compatible with composition — a contravariant functor from open sets (ordered by inclusion) to abelian groups.

---

## Sheaves

A presheaf is a **sheaf** if it additionally satisfies gluing:

1. **Separation**: sections agreeing on an open cover of $U$ are equal.
2. **Gluing**: compatible sections on an open cover of $U$ glue to a unique section on $U$.

> [!example] Sheaf of continuous functions
> $\mathcal{F}(U) = C(U, \mathbb{R})$, continuous functions on $U$, is a sheaf — continuous functions genuinely glue from compatible local pieces. Contrast with **bounded** functions: a presheaf but not a sheaf, since gluing infinitely many locally bounded pieces need not stay bounded.

---

## Stalks

The **stalk** at $x \in X$ is $\mathcal{F}_x = \varinjlim_{U \ni x} \mathcal{F}(U)$, the direct limit over shrinking neighborhoods — captures the "germ" of sections near $x$.

---

## Practice Problems

1. Verify that the constant presheaf (same group for every open set, identity restriction) fails the gluing axiom on a disconnected space.
2. Show that the sheaf of continuous functions satisfies both separation and gluing.
3. Describe the stalk of the sheaf of continuous functions at a point.
