## Pushforward and Pullback

For a continuous map $f : X \to Y$:

- **Direct image** (pushforward) $f_*\mathcal{F}$: $(f_*\mathcal{F})(V) = \mathcal{F}(f^{-1}(V))$ for $\mathcal{F}$ a sheaf on $X$.
- **Inverse image** (pullback) $f^{-1}\mathcal{G}$: the sheafification of $U \mapsto \varinjlim_{V \supseteq f(U)} \mathcal{G}(V)$ for $\mathcal{G}$ a sheaf on $Y$.

$$\text{Hom}(f^{-1}\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{F}) \cong \text{Hom}(\mathcal{G}, f_*\mathcal{F})$$

— pullback and pushforward are **adjoint functors**.

---

## Why This Matters

> [!tip] Comparing sheaf theory across spaces
> These functors let you relate sheaf cohomology on $X$ and $Y$: the **Leray spectral sequence** computes $H^n(X, \mathcal{F})$ from $H^p(Y, R^qf_*\mathcal{F})$, generalizing the Serre spectral sequence (see [[14.2.5 Spectral sequences (intro)]]) to sheaves.

## Practice Problems

1. Verify the adjunction $\text{Hom}(f^{-1}\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{F}) \cong \text{Hom}(\mathcal{G}, f_*\mathcal{F})$ in the simplest case where $f$ is the identity.
2. Explain informally what $f_*\mathcal{F}$ looks like when $f$ is the inclusion of a point.
3. Look up the Leray spectral sequence's statement and compare it to the Serre spectral sequence.
