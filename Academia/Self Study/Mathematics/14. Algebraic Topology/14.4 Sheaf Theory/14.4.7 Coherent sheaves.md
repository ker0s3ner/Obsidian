## Definition

On a scheme (or complex-analytic space) $X$ with structure sheaf $\mathcal{O}_X$, a sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_X$-modules $\mathcal{F}$ is **coherent** if it is locally finitely presented: locally, $\mathcal{F}$ fits in an exact sequence $\mathcal{O}_X^m \to \mathcal{O}_X^n \to \mathcal{F} \to 0$.

---

## Why Coherence Is the Right Finiteness Condition

> [!example] Vector bundles as coherent sheaves
> A vector bundle of rank $r$ is exactly a **locally free** coherent sheaf of rank $r$ — coherent sheaves generalize vector bundles to allow varying rank and singular behavior (e.g. an ideal sheaf of a subvariety).

Coherent sheaf cohomology on projective varieties is always finite-dimensional (Serre's finiteness theorem) — the finiteness condition that makes intersection theory and moduli problems tractable.
