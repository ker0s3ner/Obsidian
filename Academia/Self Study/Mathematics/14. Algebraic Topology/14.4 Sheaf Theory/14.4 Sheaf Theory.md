## Overview

Sheaf theory generalizes the idea of "locally defined data that glues" from continuous functions to arbitrary algebraic and geometric contexts, and provides the cohomology theory used throughout modern algebraic geometry and number theory.

> [!info] Prerequisites
> - [[14.2 Algebraic Topology II]] (cohomology, exact sequences)
> - Basic category theory ([[16. Specialized Topics/Category Theory]])
