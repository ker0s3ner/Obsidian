## Motivation

Singular spaces (varieties with bad points) don't satisfy Poincaré duality with ordinary sheaf cohomology. **Perverse sheaves** are a specially chosen subcategory of the derived category of sheaves (see [[14.4.5 Derived categories (intro)]]) on which an analogue of duality is restored.

---

## Why They Matter

> [!example] The Decomposition Theorem
> Perverse sheaves underlie the **Decomposition Theorem** (Beilinson–Bernstein–Deligne–Gabber), which describes how the derived pushforward of a nice (proper) map decomposes into simple pieces — a cornerstone of modern intersection cohomology and the geometric Langlands program.
