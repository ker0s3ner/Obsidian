## The Global Sections Functor

$\Gamma(X, -) : \mathcal{F} \mapsto \mathcal{F}(X)$ is **not exact** — a short exact sequence of sheaves need not stay exact after taking global sections. **Sheaf cohomology** $H^n(X, \mathcal{F})$ measures this failure, defined via derived functors of $\Gamma$ (injective resolutions), or equivalently via Čech cohomology on a good cover.

---

## Long Exact Sequence

A short exact sequence of sheaves $0 \to \mathcal{F}' \to \mathcal{F} \to \mathcal{F}'' \to 0$ gives a long exact sequence:

$$0 \to H^0(X,\mathcal{F}') \to H^0(X,\mathcal{F}) \to H^0(X,\mathcal{F}'') \to H^1(X,\mathcal{F}') \to \cdots$$

> [!tip] Why $H^0$ can fail to be right-exact
> $H^0(X, \mathcal{F}) = \mathcal{F}(X)$ is exactly global sections — the surjectivity failure of $\mathcal{F}(X) \to \mathcal{F}''(X)$ is precisely detected by $H^1(X, \mathcal{F}')$ becoming nonzero.
