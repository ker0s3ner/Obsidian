## Why Ordinary Topology Isn't Enough

Algebraic varieties over fields like $\mathbb{F}_p$ or $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ have too few open sets in the Zariski topology to support a useful cohomology theory (e.g. it can't see torsion). **Étale cohomology** replaces "open subset" with "étale morphism" (a more flexible, algebraic notion of local isomorphism), giving enough covers to define a good cohomology theory.

---

## Why It Matters

> [!example] The Weil conjectures
> Étale cohomology was developed by Grothendieck's school specifically to prove the **Weil conjectures**, relating point counts of varieties over finite fields to the eigenvalues of Frobenius acting on étale cohomology groups — ultimately proved by Deligne.
