## Definition

**Topological K-theory** $K^0(X)$ is the Grothendieck group of complex vector bundles over $X$: formal differences $[E] - [F]$ of bundles, modulo the relation that direct sum corresponds to addition.

$$K^0(X) = \{[E] - [F] : E, F \text{ complex vector bundles on } X\}$$

---

## Bott Periodicity

> [!example] K-theory is 2-periodic
> $K^n(X) \cong K^{n+2}(X)$ — a striking contrast with ordinary cohomology, which has no such periodicity. This follows from the homotopy equivalence $U \simeq \Omega(U/O) $-type relations in the classifying spaces of unitary groups.

K-theory is represented by the spectrum $KU$, and Bott periodicity is exactly the statement that $KU$ is a 2-periodic spectrum.
