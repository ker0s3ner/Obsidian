## From Cobordism to Cohomology Theory

The cobordism relation on manifolds (see [[13.3.9 Cobordism (intro)]]) organizes into a full generalized cohomology theory: **cobordism theory**, represented by **Thom spectra** $MO$, $MU$, $MSO$, etc., depending on the structure group (unoriented, complex, oriented).

$$MU^n(X) = [X, \Sigma^{-n} MU]$$

---

## Thom's Theorem

> [!tip] Cobordism groups computed via homotopy
> René Thom showed unoriented cobordism groups $\mathfrak{N}_*$ are computable as the homotopy groups of the Thom spectrum $MO$ — turning a geometric classification problem (which manifolds bound) into a homotopy-theoretic computation.
