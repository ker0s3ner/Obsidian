## Motivation: Stabilization

The **suspension** $\Sigma X$ (join with $S^0$, roughly "adding a dimension") gives maps $\pi_n(X) \to \pi_{n+1}(\Sigma X)$; the **Freudenthal suspension theorem** says these become isomorphisms once $n$ is large relative to the connectivity of $X$ — homotopy groups "stabilize."

---

## Spectra

A **spectrum** is a sequence of spaces $\{E_n\}$ with structure maps $\Sigma E_n \to E_{n+1}$, designed to package this stable information directly. Spectra form the natural home for **stable homotopy theory** — homotopy theory after inverting the suspension operation.

> [!example] Basic spectra
> - The **sphere spectrum** $\mathbb{S}$, built from $S^n$
> - The **Eilenberg–MacLane spectrum** $HG$, representing ordinary cohomology with coefficients $G$
