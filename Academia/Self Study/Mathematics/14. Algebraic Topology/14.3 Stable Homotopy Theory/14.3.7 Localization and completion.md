## Localization at a Prime

**Localizing** a space or spectrum at a prime $p$ isolates its $p$-primary behavior, formally by tensoring homotopy/homology groups with $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$ (integers with denominators coprime to $p$ inverted).

---

## Why Localize

> [!tip] Divide and conquer by prime
> Many computations in stable homotopy theory (e.g. the Adams spectral sequence, see [[14.3.6 The Adams spectral sequence]]) are naturally organized one prime at a time — the $p$-local and $q$-local pieces for different primes $p, q$ interact only through rational information, by the arithmetic fracture square.

**Completion** (at $p$) is the analogous operation using $p$-adic-style inverse limits rather than localization — closely related to the $p$-adic numbers of [[17.4 p-adic Analysis]].
