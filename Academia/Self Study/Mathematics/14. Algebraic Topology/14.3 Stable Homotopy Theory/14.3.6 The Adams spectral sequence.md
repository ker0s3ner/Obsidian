## Purpose

The **Adams spectral sequence** is the primary tool for computing stable homotopy groups of spheres $\pi_n^s$ (see [[14.3.2 Stable homotopy groups]]), converging from an algebraic input (Steenrod algebra cohomology) to the genuinely geometric stable homotopy groups.

$$E_2^{s,t} = \text{Ext}_{\mathcal{A}}^{s,t}(\mathbb{F}_2, \mathbb{F}_2) \Rightarrow \pi_{t-s}^s \otimes \mathbb{Z}_{(2)}$$

where $\mathcal{A}$ is the **mod-2 Steenrod algebra** of cohomology operations.

---

## Why It's Needed

> [!warning] Direct computation is intractable
> Stable homotopy groups resist direct geometric computation past very low degrees. The Adams spectral sequence converts the problem into (still hard, but tractable) homological algebra over the Steenrod algebra.
