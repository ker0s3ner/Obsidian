## Definition

The **stable homotopy groups of spheres** are:

$$\pi_n^s = \lim_{k \to \infty} \pi_{n+k}(S^k)$$

the value that $\pi_{n+k}(S^k)$ stabilizes to as $k$ grows (guaranteed to stabilize by the Freudenthal suspension theorem).

---

## Known Values

| $n$ | $\pi_n^s$ |
|-----|-----------|
| 0 | $\mathbb{Z}$ |
| 1 | $\mathbb{Z}/2$ |
| 2 | $\mathbb{Z}/2$ |
| 3 | $\mathbb{Z}/24$ |

> [!warning] Still an open frontier
> Stable homotopy groups of spheres are known explicitly only in a finite (if large) range — computing them fully remains one of the central open problems in algebraic topology, tackled via the Adams spectral sequence.
