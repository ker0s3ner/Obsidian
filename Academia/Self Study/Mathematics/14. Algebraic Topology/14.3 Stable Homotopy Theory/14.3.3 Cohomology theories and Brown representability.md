## Generalized Cohomology Theories

A **generalized cohomology theory** satisfies the Eilenberg–Steenrod axioms except the dimension axiom (which pins ordinary cohomology to a point having cohomology only in degree 0). Dropping it opens the door to K-theory, cobordism theory, and others.

---

## Brown Representability

> [!tip] Every cohomology theory comes from a spectrum
> Brown's representability theorem: every generalized cohomology theory $h^*$ on CW complexes is represented by a spectrum $E$: $h^n(X) \cong [X, E_n]$. This is why spectra (see [[14.3.1 Spectra]]) are the "correct" objects underlying stable homotopy theory.
