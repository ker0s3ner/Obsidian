## Definition

For a covering map $p : \tilde{X} \to X$, a **deck transformation** is a homeomorphism $\phi : \tilde{X} \to \tilde{X}$ with $p \circ \phi = p$ — it permutes the sheets of the cover without disturbing the projection.

Deck transformations form a group $\text{Deck}(\tilde{X}/X)$.

---

## Universal Covers

For the **universal cover** $\tilde{X}$ (simply connected), deck transformations correspond exactly to the fundamental group:

$$\text{Deck}(\tilde{X}/X) \cong \pi_1(X)$$

> [!example] Universal cover of the circle
> $\mathbb{R} \to S^1$, $t \mapsto e^{2\pi i t}$, is the universal cover; deck transformations are integer translations $t \mapsto t+n$, matching $\pi_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$.

---

## Practice Problems

1. Verify that integer translations of $\mathbb{R}$ are exactly the deck transformations of $\mathbb{R} \to S^1$.
2. Explain why the universal cover's deck group must act freely and transitively on each fiber.
3. Describe the deck transformation group of the $n$-fold cover $S^1 \to S^1$, $z \mapsto z^n$.
