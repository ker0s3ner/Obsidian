## Definition

For a group $G$, a **classifying space** $BG$ is a space whose fundamental group is $G$ and whose universal cover $EG$ is contractible — equivalently, $BG = EG/G$ for a free, contractible $G$-action.

$$\pi_1(BG) \cong G, \qquad \pi_n(BG) = 0 \text{ for } n \geq 2 \ (\text{if } G \text{ discrete})$$

---

## Why It Matters

> [!example] Classifying principal bundles
> $BG$ classifies principal $G$-bundles over any space $X$: bundles correspond (up to isomorphism) to homotopy classes of maps $X \to BG$. This is why classifying spaces are central to characteristic class theory.

**Example**: $B\mathbb{Z} \cong S^1$ (matches $\pi_1(S^1) = \mathbb{Z}$); $B(\mathbb{Z}/2) \cong \mathbb{R}P^\infty$.
