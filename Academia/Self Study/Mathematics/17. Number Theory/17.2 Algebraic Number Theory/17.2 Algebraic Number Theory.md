## Overview

Algebraic number theory studies number fields — finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}$ — and the arithmetic of their rings of integers, where unique factorization can fail and must be repaired with ideal-theoretic tools.

> [!tip] The throughline
> Everything here answers one question at increasing depth: **how does $\mathbb{Z}$'s arithmetic generalize to $\mathcal{O}_K$?** Ideals replace elements, class groups measure the gap, and class field theory eventually classifies every abelian extension in terms of this data.
