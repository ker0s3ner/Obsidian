## Overview

Number theory studies the integers — their divisibility, prime factorization, and the equations they solve. This folder moves from elementary techniques to the algebraic and analytic machinery needed for the deepest open questions in the subject.

> [!tip] How the pieces fit
> Elementary number theory asks concrete questions about $\mathbb{Z}$; algebraic number theory generalizes $\mathbb{Z}$ to $\mathcal{O}_K$; analytic number theory attacks questions about primes using complex analysis; $p$-adic analysis studies $\mathbb{Q}$ through a completely different metric. All four converge in modern research (e.g. the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem uses all four flavors at once).
