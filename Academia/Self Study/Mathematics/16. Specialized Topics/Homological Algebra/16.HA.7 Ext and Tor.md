## Ext

$\text{Ext}^i_R(A, -)$ is the derived functor of $\text{Hom}_R(A, -)$. It measures the failure of $\text{Hom}(A,-)$ to preserve exactness:

$$\text{Ext}^0_R(A, B) = \text{Hom}_R(A, B)$$

$\text{Ext}^1_R(A,B)$ classifies extensions of $B$ by $A$ — short exact sequences $0 \to B \to E \to A \to 0$ up to equivalence.

---

## Tor

$\text{Tor}_i^R(A, -)$ is the derived functor of $A \otimes_R -$:

$$\text{Tor}_0^R(A,B) = A \otimes_R B$$

$\text{Tor}_1^R(A,B) \neq 0$ detects **torsion** obstructions — e.g. $\text{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/n, \mathbb{Z}/m) \cong \mathbb{Z}/\gcd(n,m)$.

> [!tip] Flatness and projectivity
> $A$ is flat iff $\text{Tor}_1^R(A, -) = 0$; $A$ is projective iff $\text{Ext}^1_R(A, -) = 0$.

## Practice Problems

1. Compute $\text{Ext}^1_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/n, \mathbb{Z})$.
2. Compute $\text{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/2, \mathbb{Z}/4)$.
3. Show that $\text{Ext}^1_R(A,B) = 0$ for all $B$ iff $A$ is projective.
