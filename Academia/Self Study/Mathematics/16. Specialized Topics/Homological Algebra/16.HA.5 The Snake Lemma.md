## Statement

Given a commutative diagram of short exact rows in an abelian category:

$$\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
0 & \to & A & \to & B & \to & C & \to & 0 \\
  &     & \downarrow a & & \downarrow b & & \downarrow c & & \\
0 & \to & A' & \to & B' & \to & C' & \to & 0
\end{array}$$

there is an exact sequence connecting the kernels and cokernels of $a, b, c$:

$$0 \to \ker a \to \ker b \to \ker c \xrightarrow{\ \partial\ } \operatorname{coker} a \to \operatorname{coker} b \to \operatorname{coker} c \to 0$$

The map $\partial$ is the **connecting homomorphism**.

> [!example] Where it's used
> The snake lemma is the engine behind the long exact sequence in homology induced by a short exact sequence of chain complexes.

---

## Practice Problems

1. Construct the connecting map $\partial$ explicitly by diagram chasing.
2. Use the snake lemma to derive the long exact sequence of homology groups.
3. Show that if $a, c$ are isomorphisms, so is $b$ (the "short five lemma" as a corollary).
