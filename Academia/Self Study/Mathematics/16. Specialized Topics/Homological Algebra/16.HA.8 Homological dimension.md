## Projective and Global Dimension

The **projective dimension** of a module $A$ is the length of the shortest projective resolution:

$$\text{pd}(A) = \sup\{i : \text{Ext}^i_R(A, B) \neq 0 \text{ for some } B\}$$

The **global dimension** of a ring $R$ is $\sup_A \text{pd}(A)$ over all $R$-modules $A$.

| Ring | Global dimension |
|------|-------------------|
| Field | 0 |
| PID (e.g. $\mathbb{Z}$) | $\leq 1$ |
| Polynomial ring $k[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ | $n$ |

---

## Regular Rings

A Noetherian local ring is **regular** iff it has finite global dimension — this is the homological characterization behind the Auslander–Buchsbaum–Serre theorem, linking commutative algebra geometry to homological invariants.
