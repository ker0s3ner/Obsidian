## Motivation

A functor $F$ between abelian categories may fail to preserve exactness. **Derived functors** $R^i F$ (or $L_i F$) measure this failure, systematically extending $F$ into a sequence of functors indexed by $i \geq 0$.

---

## Construction

For a left-exact functor $F$, take an object $A$, choose an **injective resolution** $A \to I^0 \to I^1 \to \cdots$, apply $F$, and take cohomology:

$$R^i F(A) = H^i(F(I^0) \to F(I^1) \to \cdots)$$

This is independent of the resolution chosen, up to canonical isomorphism.

> [!note] Dual construction
> Right-exact functors are derived using **projective resolutions** instead, producing $L_i F$, decreasing in degree.

---

## Long Exact Sequence

A short exact sequence $0 \to A \to B \to C \to 0$ induces a long exact sequence in derived functors:

$$0 \to F(A) \to F(B) \to F(C) \to R^1F(A) \to R^1F(B) \to \cdots$$

---

## Practice Problems

1. Show $R^0 F = F$ for a left-exact functor $F$.
2. Explain why derived functors are independent of the chosen resolution.
3. Use the long exact sequence to compute $R^1 F(C)$ knowing $R^1F(A) = 0$.
