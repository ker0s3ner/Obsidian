## Smooth Actions

A Lie group $G$ **acts smoothly** on a manifold $M$ if there is a smooth map $G \times M \to M$, $(g,x) \mapsto g\cdot x$, compatible with the group operation. Key notions:

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Orbit $G \cdot x$ | All points reachable from $x$ |
| Stabilizer $G_x$ | $\{g : g \cdot x = x\}$ |
| Free action | All stabilizers trivial |
| Proper action | Preimages of compact sets under $(g,x)\mapsto(gx,x)$ are compact |

---

## Orbit–Stabilizer for Lie Groups

Each orbit is itself a homogeneous space:

$$G \cdot x \cong G / G_x$$

matching the discrete orbit–stabilizer theorem but now in the smooth category (see [[16.LG.7 Homogeneous spaces]]).

## Infinitesimal Generators

Differentiating the action gives a Lie algebra homomorphism $\mathfrak{g} \to \Gamma(TM)$, sending $X \in \mathfrak{g}$ to the vector field generating its flow — the **fundamental vector field**.

> [!tip] Moment maps
> When $M$ is symplectic and the action preserves the symplectic form, these fundamental vector fields often integrate to a **moment map**, a central object in symplectic geometry (see [[4. Geometry/4.12 Symplectic Geometry]]).

## Practice Problems

1. Show that the orbit map $G/G_x \to G\cdot x$ is a diffeomorphism when the action is proper and free.
2. Compute the fundamental vector field of the $SO(2)$ rotation action on $\mathbb{R}^2$.
