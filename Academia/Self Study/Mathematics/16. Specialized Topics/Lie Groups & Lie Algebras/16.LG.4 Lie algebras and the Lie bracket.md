## Definition

A **Lie algebra** $\mathfrak{g}$ is a vector space with a bilinear bracket $[\cdot,\cdot]: \mathfrak{g} \times \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g}$ satisfying:

$$[X,Y] = -[Y,X], \qquad [X,[Y,Z]] + [Y,[Z,X]] + [Z,[X,Y]] = 0 \ \text{(Jacobi identity)}$$

For a Lie group $G$, its Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g} = T_eG$ arises as the tangent space at the identity, with $[X,Y]$ defined via the commutator of left-invariant vector fields.

---

## Matrix Example

For $G = GL(n,\mathbb{R})$, $\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb{R})$ is all $n \times n$ matrices with bracket:

$$[X,Y] = XY - YX$$

---

## Adjoint Representation

$G$ acts on $\mathfrak{g}$ via $\text{Ad}(g) = d(c_g)_e$, where $c_g(h) = ghg^{-1}$. Its derivative at the identity gives:

$$\text{ad}(X)(Y) = [X,Y]$$

> [!tip] Why the bracket matters
> $[X,Y]$ measures the failure of the flows of $X$ and $Y$ to commute — it's the infinitesimal shadow of the group's non-abelian structure.

## Practice Problems

1. Verify the Jacobi identity for $\mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb{R})$ with the commutator bracket.
2. Compute the Lie algebra of $SO(3)$ and its bracket.
3. Show $\text{ad}(X)$ is a derivation: $\text{ad}(X)[Y,Z] = [\text{ad}(X)Y, Z] + [Y, \text{ad}(X)Z]$.
