## Definition

A **homogeneous space** is a manifold $M$ on which a Lie group $G$ acts transitively. Fixing a point $x_0$ with stabilizer (isotropy group) $H = \{g \in G : g \cdot x_0 = x_0\}$, there is a diffeomorphism:

$$M \cong G/H$$

---

## Examples

| $G$ | $H$ | $G/H$ |
|-----|-----|-------|
| $SO(n+1)$ | $SO(n)$ | Sphere $S^n$ |
| $GL(n)$ | Stabilizer of a $k$-plane | Grassmannian $\text{Gr}(k,n)$ |
| $SU(2)$ | $U(1)$ | $S^2$ |

---

## Invariant Structures

Because $G$ acts transitively, any $G$-invariant tensor at $x_0$ (metric, symplectic form) extends uniquely to all of $M$ — this is how many symmetric Riemannian metrics are constructed.

> [!note] Symmetric spaces
> When additionally there's an involution fixing $x_0$ with derivative $-1$ on the tangent space, $G/H$ is a **symmetric space** — e.g. spheres, hyperbolic space, and Grassmannians.

## Practice Problems

1. Verify $S^2 \cong SU(2)/U(1)$ by identifying the stabilizer of a point.
2. Show that the real projective space $\mathbb{RP}^n$ is a homogeneous space of $SO(n+1)$.
3. Explain why $G/H$ inherits a well-defined tangent space at each point from $\mathfrak{g}/\mathfrak{h}$.
