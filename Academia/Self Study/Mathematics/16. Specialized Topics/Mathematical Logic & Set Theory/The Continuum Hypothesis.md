## Statement

The **Continuum Hypothesis (CH)** asserts there is no cardinal strictly between $\aleph_0$ and $2^{\aleph_0}$:

$$2^{\aleph_0} = \aleph_1$$

The **Generalized Continuum Hypothesis (GCH)** extends this to every infinite cardinal: $2^{\aleph_\alpha} = \aleph_{\alpha+1}$.

---

## Independence

Gödel (1940) showed CH is **consistent** with ZFC (via the constructible universe $L$); Cohen (1963) showed $\neg$CH is **also consistent** (via forcing). Together:

$$\text{CH is independent of ZFC}$$

— neither CH nor its negation can be proved from the ZFC axioms.

> [!tip] What "independent" means
> ZFC simply doesn't settle the question. Mathematicians can consistently work in a universe where CH holds or one where it fails; both are equally "valid" set theories.
