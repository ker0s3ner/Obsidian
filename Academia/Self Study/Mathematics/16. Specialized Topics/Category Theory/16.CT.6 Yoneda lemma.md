## Statement

For a category $\mathcal{C}$, an object $A \in \mathcal{C}$, and a functor $F : \mathcal{C} \to \mathbf{Set}$, there is a natural bijection:

$$\text{Nat}(\text{Hom}(A, -), F) \cong F(A)$$

given by sending a natural transformation $\eta$ to $\eta_A(\text{id}_A) \in F(A)$.

---

## Yoneda Embedding

Taking $F = \text{Hom}(B, -)$ gives:

$$\text{Nat}(\text{Hom}(A,-), \text{Hom}(B,-)) \cong \text{Hom}(B, A)$$

so the functor $A \mapsto \text{Hom}(A, -)$ is **fully faithful** — an object is completely determined, up to isomorphism, by its Hom-sets.

> [!tip] Slogan
> An object is exactly what it does — knowing all morphisms into (or out of) $A$ is the same as knowing $A$.

## Practice Problems

1. Verify that $\eta_A(\text{id}_A) \in F(A)$ determines $\eta$ uniquely.
2. Use the Yoneda embedding to show two objects with isomorphic Hom-functors are isomorphic.
3. Apply the Yoneda lemma to representable functors in $\mathbf{Set}$.
