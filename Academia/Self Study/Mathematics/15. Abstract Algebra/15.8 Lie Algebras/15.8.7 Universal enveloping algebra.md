## Definition

The **universal enveloping algebra** $U(\mathfrak g)$ is the associative algebra generated by $\mathfrak g$, subject to $xy - yx = [x,y]$ for $x,y \in \mathfrak g$ — turning bracket relations into associative relations.

---

## Universal Property

Representations of $\mathfrak g$ correspond exactly to $U(\mathfrak g)$-modules — the enveloping algebra converts Lie theory into ordinary (associative) representation theory.

## PBW Theorem

The **Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem**: if $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ is a basis of $\mathfrak g$, then ordered monomials $x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_n^{a_n}$ form a basis of $U(\mathfrak g)$ — so $U(\mathfrak g)$ is "as big as expected," with no unexpected relations.

> [!tip] Casimir element
> $U(\mathfrak g)$ contains the **Casimir element** (built from the Killing form), which acts as a scalar on each irreducible representation — a key invariant for classifying and distinguishing representations.
