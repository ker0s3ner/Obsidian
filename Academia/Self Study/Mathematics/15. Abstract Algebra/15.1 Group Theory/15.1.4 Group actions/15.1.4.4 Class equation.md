## Conjugacy Classes

$G$ acts on itself by conjugation: $g \cdot x = gxg^{-1}$. The orbits are **conjugacy classes**. By orbit-stabilizer, the size of the conjugacy class of $x$ is:

$$|\text{Cl}(x)| = |G : C_G(x)|$$

where $C_G(x)$ is the centralizer of $x$.

---

## Class Equation

Summing class sizes over distinct conjugacy classes (representatives $x_1, \ldots, x_k$):

$$|G| = \sum_{i=1}^{k} |G : C_G(x_i)|$$

Central elements ($x \in Z(G)$) form singleton classes, so this is often split as:

$$|G| = |Z(G)| + \sum_{x_i \notin Z(G)} |G : C_G(x_i)|$$

> [!example] Application — $p$-groups have nontrivial center
> If $|G| = p^n$, every term $|G:C_G(x_i)|$ for $x_i \notin Z(G)$ is divisible by $p$ (it's a proper power of $p$ dividing $|G|$), forcing $|Z(G)| \equiv 0 \pmod p$, so $Z(G) \neq \{e\}$.

## Practice Problems

1. Write the class equation for $S_4$.
2. Prove that every group of order $p^2$ is abelian using the class equation.
3. Show that a group of prime-power order has nontrivial center.
4. Compute the conjugacy classes of $D_4$ and verify the class equation.
5. Use the class equation to show a group of order 6 with trivial center cannot exist.
