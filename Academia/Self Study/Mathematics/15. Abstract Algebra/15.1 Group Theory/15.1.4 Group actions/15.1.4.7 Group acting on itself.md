## Three Natural Actions of $G$ on $G$

| Action | Formula | Faithful? | Stabilizer of $x$ |
|--------|---------|-----------|---------------------|
| Left multiplication | $g \cdot x = gx$ | Always | $\{e\}$ (free action) |
| Right multiplication | $g \cdot x = xg^{-1}$ | Always | $\{e\}$ |
| Conjugation | $g \cdot x = gxg^{-1}$ | Not always | $C_G(x)$ |

---

## Left Multiplication and Cayley's Theorem

Left multiplication is always free and faithful, giving the embedding $G \hookrightarrow \text{Sym}(G)$ from [[15.1.1 Groups/15.1.1.8 Cayley's theorem]].

## Conjugation and the Center

Conjugation is faithful iff $Z(G) = \{e\}$ — since the kernel of the conjugation homomorphism $G \to \text{Sym}(G)$ is exactly $Z(G)$.

> [!note] Why three actions, one group
> These give different information: left/right multiplication reveal $G$'s size structurally (Cayley), while conjugation reveals $G$'s internal symmetry (conjugacy classes, center).

## Practice Problems

1. Show the kernel of the conjugation action is $Z(G)$.
2. Verify left multiplication is a free action.
3. Show right multiplication by $g$ equals left multiplication by $g^{-1}$ composed with inversion — are they "the same" action?
4. For $G = S_3$, is conjugation faithful?
5. Explain why left multiplication cannot fix any non-identity element.
