## Definition

The **dihedral group** $D_n$ is the symmetry group of a regular $n$-gon: rotations and reflections, with $|D_n| = 2n$.

$$D_n = \langle r, s \mid r^n = s^2 = e, \ srs = r^{-1} \rangle$$

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| $r$ | rotation by $2\pi/n$ |
| $s$ | a reflection |

---

## Structure

Every element of $D_n$ is uniquely $r^k$ or $sr^k$ for $0 \leq k < n$. $D_n$ is **non-abelian** for $n \geq 3$, since $sr \neq rs$ in general.

> [!example] $D_3 \cong S_3$
> The symmetries of an equilateral triangle are exactly all permutations of its 3 vertices — so $D_3$ and $S_3$ are isomorphic, both of order 6.

---

## Subgroups

The rotations $\langle r \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ form a normal subgroup of index 2 — the reflections are not closed under composition (composing two reflections gives a rotation).

## Practice Problems

1. Write out the multiplication table for $D_4$ (symmetries of the square).
2. Show that $\langle r \rangle$ is a normal subgroup of $D_n$.
3. Verify the relation $srs = r^{-1}$ geometrically for $D_4$.
4. Show that $D_3 \cong S_3$ by exhibiting an explicit isomorphism.
5. Is $D_n$ ever abelian? For which $n$?
