## Definition

Given groups $G$ and $H$, the **direct product** $G \times H$ is the set of ordered pairs $(g, h)$ with componentwise operation:

$$(g_1, h_1)(g_2, h_2) = (g_1 g_2, h_1 h_2)$$

$|G \times H| = |G| \cdot |H|$, and $G \times H$ is abelian iff both $G$ and $H$ are.

---

## Structure Theorem for Finite Abelian Groups

Every finite abelian group decomposes as a direct product of cyclic groups of prime-power order:

$$G \cong \mathbb{Z}/p_1^{a_1}\mathbb{Z} \times \cdots \times \mathbb{Z}/p_k^{a_k}\mathbb{Z}$$

> [!tip] This classifies ALL finite abelian groups
> Two finite abelian groups are isomorphic iff they have the same multiset of prime-power factors — a complete classification.

---

## When is $\mathbb{Z}/mn\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$?

By the **Chinese Remainder Theorem**, this holds exactly when $\gcd(m,n) = 1$.

**Example**: $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$, but $\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z} \not\cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ (the latter has no element of order 4).

## Practice Problems

1. List all abelian groups of order 12 up to isomorphism.
2. Show that $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$ explicitly.
3. Prove $G \times H$ is abelian iff both $G$ and $H$ are abelian.
4. Show $\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z} \not\cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ by comparing element orders.
5. How many abelian groups of order $p^3$ are there, for prime $p$?
