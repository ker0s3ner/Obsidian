## Definition

For subgroups $H, K \leq G$ and $g \in G$, the **double coset** is:

$$HgK = \{hgk : h \in H, k \in K\}$$

Double cosets partition $G$, generalizing ordinary (single) cosets — recovered by taking $K = \{e\}$.

---

## Size of a Double Coset

$$|HgK| = \frac{|H| \cdot |K|}{|H \cap gKg^{-1}|}$$

---

## Connection to Group Actions

Double coset decomposition $H \backslash G / K$ describes the orbits of $H$ acting on the coset space $G/K$ by left multiplication — a direct application of the orbit-stabilizer framework from [[15.1.4 Group actions/15.1.4.1 Orbits and stabilizers]].

> [!note] Where this appears
> Double cosets are central to the theory of **Hecke operators** in number theory and to **Bruhat decomposition** in Lie theory — both organize a group into double-coset "cells."

## Practice Problems

1. Compute the double cosets $H \backslash S_3 / K$ for $H = K = \langle (1\,2) \rangle$.
2. Prove that double cosets partition $G$.
3. Verify the size formula $|HgK| = |H||K|/|H \cap gKg^{-1}|$ on a small example.
4. Show that ordinary left cosets are the special case $K = \{e\}$.
