## Definition

For $H \leq G$ and $g \in G$, the **left coset** of $H$ is $gH = \{gh : h \in H\}$; the **right coset** is $Hg = \{hg : h \in H\}$.

---

## Coset Partition

The left cosets of $H$ partition $G$: any two cosets are either identical or disjoint, and every element of $G$ lies in exactly one.

$$gH = g'H \iff g^{-1}g' \in H$$

> [!warning] Left ≠ right in general
> $gH$ and $Hg$ need not coincide unless $H$ is normal (see [[15.1.3 Normal subgroups/15.1.3.1 Quotient groups]]). This asymmetry disappears precisely for normal subgroups.

---

## All Cosets Have the Same Size

The map $h \mapsto gh$ is a bijection $H \to gH$, so $|gH| = |H|$ for every $g$ — the fact underlying Lagrange's theorem.

---

## Example

In $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$ with $H = \{0, 3\}$, the cosets are $0 + H = \{0,3\}$, $1+H = \{1,4\}$, $2+H = \{2,5\}$ — three disjoint cosets covering all 6 elements.

---

## Practice Problems

1. List all left cosets of $H = \{0, 2, 4\}$ in $\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}$.
2. Prove $gH = g'H \iff g^{-1}g' \in H$.
3. Show that $|gH| = |H|$ for every $g \in G$.
4. Find a subgroup $H \leq S_3$ where left and right cosets differ.
5. Prove that distinct cosets are disjoint.
