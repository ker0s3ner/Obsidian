## Definition

The **commutator** of $a, b \in G$ is $[a,b] = a^{-1}b^{-1}ab$. The **commutator subgroup** $[G,G]$ is generated by all commutators:

$$[G,G] = \langle [a,b] : a, b \in G \rangle$$

$[G,G] \trianglelefteq G$ always, and $G/[G,G]$ is the largest abelian quotient of $G$ — the **abelianization**.

---

## Key Property

$[a,b] = e$ for all $a,b$ iff $G$ is abelian, so $[G,G] = \{e\}$ exactly when $G$ is abelian.

> [!tip] Universal abelian quotient
> Any homomorphism $G \to A$ with $A$ abelian factors uniquely through $G/[G,G]$ — abelianization is the "closest abelian approximation" to $G$.

---

## Example

$[S_n, S_n] = A_n$ for $n \geq 2$: the abelianization of $S_n$ is $S_n/A_n \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$.

## Practice Problems

1. Show $[G,G] = \{e\}$ iff $G$ is abelian.
2. Prove $[G,G] \trianglelefteq G$.
3. Verify $[S_3, S_3] = A_3$ by computing a few commutators directly.
4. Show that $G/[G,G]$ is abelian.
5. Compute the commutator subgroup of $D_4$.
