## Definition

An algebraic extension $K/F$ is **separable** if the minimal polynomial of every $\alpha \in K$ has no repeated roots (in an algebraic closure).

---

## Characteristic Zero Is Automatic

In characteristic 0, every algebraic extension is separable — a nonconstant irreducible polynomial and its derivative are automatically coprime.

## Characteristic $p$ and Perfect Fields

In characteristic $p$, inseparability can occur: e.g. $x^p - t$ over $\mathbb{F}_p(t)$ has the single repeated root $t^{1/p}$. A field where this never happens (every algebraic extension is separable) is **perfect** — all finite fields and all fields of characteristic 0 are perfect.

> [!warning] The classic inseparable example
> $K = \mathbb{F}_p(t^{1/p})$ over $F = \mathbb{F}_p(t)$: the minimal polynomial $x^p - t$ has $(x - t^{1/p})^p$ as its factorization over $K$ — a single root with multiplicity $p$.

## Practice Problems

1. Show $x^p - t$ factors as $(x-t^{1/p})^p$ over $\mathbb{F}_p(t^{1/p})$.
2. Prove every algebraic extension in characteristic 0 is separable.
3. Show every finite field is perfect.
4. Explain why $\gcd(f,f')=1$ tests separability of $f$.
5. Is $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[3]{2})/\mathbb{Q}$ separable?
