## Partitions and Young Diagrams

Irreducible representations of $S_n$ are indexed exactly by **partitions** of $n$, visualized as **Young diagrams** — rows of boxes with weakly decreasing lengths summing to $n$.

**Example**: $S_3$ has partitions $3$, $2+1$, $1+1+1$, giving the trivial, standard (2-dim), and sign representations — matching the 3 irreducibles found via character theory.

---

## Standard Young Tableaux and Dimension

The dimension of the irreducible representation for partition $\lambda$ equals the number of **standard Young tableaux** of shape $\lambda$ (fill boxes with $1,\ldots,n$, increasing along rows and columns), computable via the **hook length formula**.

> [!example] Hook length formula
> $$\dim V_\lambda = \frac{n!}{\prod_{\text{boxes}} \text{hook length}}$$

## Practice Problems

1. List all partitions of 4 and their Young diagrams.
2. Use the hook length formula to compute the dimension of the irreducible for partition $(2,2)$ of $S_4$.
3. Identify the sign and trivial representations with specific partitions.
4. Count standard Young tableaux of shape $(2,1)$ directly and compare to the character-theory dimension for $S_3$.
5. Verify $\sum_\lambda (\dim V_\lambda)^2 = n!$ for $n=3$.
