## Definition

The **character** of a representation $\rho: G \to GL(V)$ is $\chi_\rho(g) = \operatorname{tr}(\rho(g))$.

Characters are **conjugation-invariant** (class functions): $\chi_\rho(hgh^{-1}) = \chi_\rho(g)$, since trace is invariant under conjugation of matrices.

---

## Key Properties

- $\chi_\rho(e) = \dim V$
- Isomorphic representations have equal characters
- Characters determine the representation up to isomorphism (over $\mathbb{C}$)

> [!example] Character table
> For a finite group with $k$ conjugacy classes, there are exactly $k$ irreducible characters, organized into a square **character table** — one of the most information-dense objects in finite group theory.

## Practice Problems

1. Compute the character of the regular representation of $\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$.
2. Show $\chi_\rho(e) = \dim V$.
3. Verify characters are class functions using the trace identity $\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)$.
4. Build the character table of $\mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}$.
5. Show that two representations with the same character are isomorphic (state as fact if proof is deferred).
