## Definition

Given $H \leq G$ and a representation $W$ of $H$, the **induced representation** $\text{Ind}_H^G(W)$ is a representation of $G$ built from $W$, of dimension $\dim W \cdot |G:H|$.

Concretely: $\text{Ind}_H^G(W) = \mathbb{C}[G] \otimes_{\mathbb{C}[H]} W$.

---

## Frobenius Reciprocity

$$\langle \text{Ind}_H^G(\chi), \psi \rangle_G = \langle \chi, \text{Res}_H^G(\psi)\rangle_H$$

Induction and **restriction** (viewing a $G$-representation as an $H$-representation by forgetting the rest of the action) are adjoint operations.

> [!tip] Building representations "from below"
> Inducing from small subgroups is a standard way to construct irreducible representations of $G$ when direct construction is hard — this is central to representation theory of symmetric and Lie groups.

## Practice Problems

1. Compute $\text{Ind}_{\{e\}}^G(\text{trivial})$ — show it's the regular representation.
2. Verify the dimension formula $\dim \text{Ind}_H^G(W) = \dim W \cdot |G:H|$.
3. State Frobenius reciprocity and explain the adjoint relationship in words.
4. Compute $\text{Res}_{A_3}^{S_3}$ of the 2-dimensional irreducible of $S_3$.
5. Show inducing the trivial representation from $H$ gives the permutation representation on $G/H$.
