## Tor

$\text{Tor}_i^R(M,N)$ is the derived functor of $M\otimes_R-$. $\text{Tor}_0 = M\otimes N$; higher $\text{Tor}_i$ measure the failure of $\otimes$ to preserve injections.

**Example**: $\text{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}/m\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/\gcd(m,n)\mathbb{Z}$ — nonzero exactly when the modules share torsion.

---

## Ext

$\text{Ext}_R^i(M,N)$ is the derived functor of $\text{Hom}_R(-,N)$. $\text{Ext}^0 = \text{Hom}(M,N)$; $\text{Ext}^1(M,N)$ classifies extensions:

$$0 \to N \to E \to M \to 0$$

up to equivalence, with the zero class corresponding to the split extension.

> [!tip] Vanishing criteria
> $M$ is projective iff $\text{Ext}^i(M,-)=0$ for $i\geq1$; $N$ is injective iff $\text{Ext}^i(-,N)=0$ for $i\geq 1$; $M$ is flat iff $\text{Tor}_i(M,-)=0$ for $i\geq1$.

## Practice Problems

1. Compute $\text{Tor}_1^{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z})$.
2. Show $\text{Ext}^1_{\mathbb{Z}}(\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ and interpret via extensions.
3. Explain what a nonzero class in $\text{Ext}^1(M,N)$ means concretely.
4. Show $\text{Tor}_i(M,N)=0$ for all $i\geq1$ if $M$ is free.
5. Verify $\text{Ext}^0(M,N) = \text{Hom}(M,N)$ from the resolution definition.
