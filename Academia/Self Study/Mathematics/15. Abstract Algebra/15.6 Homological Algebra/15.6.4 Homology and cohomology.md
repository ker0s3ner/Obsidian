## From Topology to Algebra

Homological algebra abstracts the chain-complex machinery of algebraic topology (see [[14. Algebraic Topology/14.2 Algebraic Topology II/14.2.1 Homology]]) into a purely algebraic setting — the same computations (kernels mod images) apply whether $C_\bullet$ comes from a simplicial complex or a projective resolution.

---

## Cohomology as a Dual Notion

Where homology tracks "cycles modulo boundaries," cohomology (arrows reversed, upper indices) often carries **extra structure** — a ring structure via cup product, functoriality that's contravariant rather than covariant.

> [!example] de Rham cohomology
> On a smooth manifold, differential forms with $d^2=0$ give a cochain complex whose cohomology (de Rham cohomology) agrees with topological (simplicial/singular) cohomology — an algebraic-analytic bridge (de Rham's theorem).

---

## Universal Coefficient Theorem (flavor)

Cohomology with coefficients in $N$ relates to homology via Ext and Tor — a direct application of the derived functors from [[15.6.3 Ext and Tor]] to topology.
