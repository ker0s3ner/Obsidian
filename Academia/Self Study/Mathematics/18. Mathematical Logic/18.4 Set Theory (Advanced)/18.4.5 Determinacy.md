## Infinite Games

For $A \subseteq \{0,1\}^{\mathbb N}$, define a game where two players alternately pick bits $x_0, x_1, x_2, \ldots$; Player I wins iff the resulting sequence $x \in A$. The game is **determined** if one player has a winning strategy.

---

## Axiom of Determinacy vs Choice

> [!warning] AD contradicts full Choice
> The **Axiom of Determinacy** (every such game is determined) is inconsistent with the full Axiom of Choice, since Choice constructs non-determined games via a transfinite selection process. AD is instead studied as an alternative axiom, especially restricted to definable sets.

---

## Borel and Analytic Determinacy

- **Borel Determinacy** (Martin): every game with a Borel payoff set $A$ is determined — provable in ZFC alone
- **Analytic Determinacy**: requires the existence of a measurable cardinal (see [[18.4.3 Large cardinals]]) — determinacy for more complex definable sets costs large cardinal strength

This exact trade — more definable determinacy in exchange for more consistency strength — mirrors the regularity-property boundary from [[18.4.4 Descriptive set theory]].
