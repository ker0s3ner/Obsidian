## The Independence Problem

Some statements — like the **Continuum Hypothesis** (CH): $2^{\aleph_0} = \aleph_1$ — are **independent** of ZFC: neither provable nor refutable from the axioms. **Forcing**, invented by Cohen (1963), is the technique for proving independence.

---

## Idea

Starting from a model $M$ of ZFC, forcing constructs a **larger** model $M[G]$ by adjoining a carefully chosen "generic" object $G$ — analogous to how a field extension adjoins a new element, but controlled by a partial order of "conditions" that approximate $G$.

> [!example] Cohen's Result
> Using forcing, Cohen built a model of ZFC where CH **fails**. Combined with Gödel's earlier constructible universe $L$ (where CH **holds**), this shows CH is independent of ZFC.

---

## Generic Extensions

A **forcing poset** $\mathbb{P}$ consists of conditions ordered by "more informative than." A **generic filter** $G \subseteq \mathbb{P}$ meets every dense subset definable in $M$, and $M[G]$ is built from $M$-definable "names" evaluated using $G$.
