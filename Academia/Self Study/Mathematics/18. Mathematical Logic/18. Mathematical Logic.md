## Overview

Mathematical logic studies formal languages, proof, truth, and computation as mathematical objects in their own right — asking not just what's true, but what's *provable*, *definable*, or *decidable*.

> [!tip] How the pieces fit
> Model theory studies the semantic side (truth in structures); proof theory the syntactic side (formal derivability) — Gödel's completeness theorem ([[18.1.2 Completeness theorem]]) shows they agree for first-order logic. Computability theory shows some questions have no algorithm at all, and set theory shows some questions have no proof at all, from ZFC.
