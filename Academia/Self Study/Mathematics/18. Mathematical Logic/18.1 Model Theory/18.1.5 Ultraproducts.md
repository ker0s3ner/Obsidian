## Ultrafilters

An **ultrafilter** $U$ on a set $I$ is a maximal filter: for every $X \subseteq I$, exactly one of $X, I\setminus X$ is in $U$.

---

## Ultraproduct Construction

Given structures $\{\mathcal M_i\}_{i \in I}$ and an ultrafilter $U$ on $I$, the **ultraproduct** $\prod_U \mathcal M_i$ is the product $\prod_i M_i$ modulo the equivalence $f \sim g \iff \{i : f(i)=g(i)\} \in U$ — "agreeing almost everywhere, relative to $U$."

---

## Łoś's Theorem

> [!example] Łoś's Theorem
> $$\prod_U \mathcal M_i \models \varphi[\bar f] \iff \{i \in I : \mathcal M_i \models \varphi[\bar f(i)]\} \in U$$

A first-order sentence holds in the ultraproduct iff it holds on a $U$-large set of coordinates — this gives a slick, algebraic proof of the Compactness Theorem, and underlies nonstandard analysis (constructing hyperreal numbers as an ultraproduct of $\mathbb R$).

## Practice Problems

1. Explain why an ultrafilter must decide every subset of $I$, unlike an ordinary filter.
2. Use Łoś's theorem to argue why an ultraproduct of fields is again a field.
3. Sketch how an ultraproduct of $\mathbb{R}$ (over a nonprincipal ultrafilter on $\mathbb N$) produces infinitesimal elements.
