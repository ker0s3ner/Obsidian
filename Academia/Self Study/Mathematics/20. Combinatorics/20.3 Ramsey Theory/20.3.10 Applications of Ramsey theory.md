## Theoretical Computer Science

Ramsey-type results underlie lower bounds in **communication complexity** and **property testing** — a monochromatic substructure often corresponds to a "confusable" pair of inputs that no efficient protocol can distinguish.

---

## Geometry: Happy Ending Problem

Erdős and Szekeres showed any sufficiently large set of points in general position in the plane contains a subset in **convex position** forming a convex $n$-gon — a genuinely geometric Ramsey-type statement, proved using the same finite-Ramsey-number machinery.

---

## Number Theory

Van der Waerden's and Rado's theorems ([[20.3.2 Van der Waerden theorem]], [[20.3.5 Rado's theorem]]) directly govern which linear-equation and progression patterns must appear in any coloring of the integers — a recurring tool in additive number theory.

> [!tip] The unifying moral
> Ramsey theory's central insight — "any sufficiently large or structured system contains order" — recurs across combinatorics, logic, geometry, and computer science whenever a problem has a coloring/partition flavor.
