## Combinatorial Optimization

Matroids abstract exactly which independence systems admit efficient exact optimization: minimum spanning trees (graphic matroids, [[20.5.6 Greedy algorithm and matroids]]), and more generally any problem reducible to matroid intersection or matroid union.

---

## Network Coding and Rigidity

Matroids representable over specific fields govern achievable rates in network coding, and **rigidity matroids** (independence = infinitesimal rigidity of bar-joint frameworks) determine when a mechanical structure is rigid vs. flexible.

---

## Algebraic Geometry: Matroids from Hyperplane Arrangements

Every finite arrangement of hyperplanes gives rise to a matroid recording linear dependencies among the defining vectors — connecting matroid theory to characteristic polynomials of arrangements and to [[4. Geometry/4.13 Algebraic Geometry (Classical)]].

> [!tip] Why matroids recur everywhere
> Any time a problem reduces to "which subsets are independent, subject to an exchange property," matroid theory supplies both a classification language and ready-made algorithms (greedy, intersection) for free.
