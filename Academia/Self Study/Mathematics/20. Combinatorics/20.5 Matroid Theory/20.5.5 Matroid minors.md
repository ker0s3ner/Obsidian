## Deletion and Contraction

For a matroid $M=(E,\mathcal{I})$ and element $e \in E$:

- **Deletion** $M \setminus e$: restrict to $E\setminus\{e\}$, keeping independent sets that avoid $e$.
- **Contraction** $M / e$: independent sets of $M/e$ are $\{I \subseteq E\setminus\{e\} : I\cup\{e\} \in \mathcal{I}\}$ (when $e$ is not a loop).

A **minor** of $M$ is obtained by any sequence of deletions and contractions.

---

## Duality Interacts with Minors

$$(M \setminus e)^* = M^* / e, \qquad (M/e)^* = M^* \setminus e$$

so deletion and contraction are dual operations, swapped by matroid duality ([[20.5.2 Duality]]) — directly generalizing how deleting/contracting an edge in a planar graph corresponds to contracting/deleting the corresponding edge in its planar dual.

> [!tip] Forbidden minor characterizations
> Just as Kuratowski's theorem characterizes planar graphs by forbidden minors ($K_5$, $K_{3,3}$), many matroid classes (e.g. graphic matroids) are characterized by finite lists of forbidden minors.

## Practice Problems

1. Compute $M\setminus e$ and $M/e$ explicitly for a small graphic matroid and a chosen edge $e$.
2. Verify the duality-minor interaction formula on a small example.
3. Explain the graph-theoretic meaning of deletion and contraction for graphic matroids.
